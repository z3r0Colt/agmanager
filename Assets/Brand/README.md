# Davey Jones' Locker brand assets

`logo-mark.png` is the transparent 1254 × 1254 master emblem. The pirate flag,
gaming PC, and brass porthole preserve the identity of the original application
icon. Small lettering and the banner were removed for legibility.

`../../appico.ico` is the Windows application icon derived from the master. It
contains transparent 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96, 128, and 256 px frames.
The executable, WPF window/taskbar, and system tray share this icon. The sidebar,
title bar, and About page use the PNG master as a WPF resource.

Artwork was created with the built-in image generation tool, using the original
`appico.ico` as the edit target. The PNG was only resampled for ICO packaging;
the original generated transparency was preserved.

## Final artwork prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: the supplied existing Davey's Locker Windows app icon. Create its professionally redesigned standalone emblem, preserving its specific nautical gaming identity: a pirate flag bearing a skull at left, a gaming desktop PC tower with cyan lighting at right, inside a circular brass porthole. Simplify aggressively into a strong memorable brand mark with clean vector-like contours and carefully balanced negative space, bold shapes and minimal internal detail that will remain legible when downscaled to 32px. Keep the flag and PC equally recognizable. Palette drawn from existing artwork: near-black navy, restrained warm brass/gold outer ring and off-white skull, luminous cyan PC accents, NO new palette. Use one confident broad brass ring with only a few rivets; eliminate thin ropes, compass, treasure, background scene, sparks, small cables, texture noise, flourishes. Pirate flag has a large stylized skull and just two simple crossed bones. PC is an upright simplified tower with two big cyan circles and a clean cyan edge. Premium flat/enamel logo treatment, precise symmetrical outer silhouette, balanced asymmetric center. Absolutely no text, lettering, ribbons or banners. Do not reproduce the current misspelled banner. Single centered circular emblem occupying about 88 percent of the square canvas, transparent outside its silhouette; navy negative space inside emblem. Clean antialiased edges, no external glow, no cast shadow, no mockup, no background rectangle. Deliver a finished master logo mark, not a concept sheet.
